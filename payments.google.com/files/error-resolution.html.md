Section 4. EFT Error Resolution Policy

This Section 4 - EFT Error Resolution Policy only applies to Google Pay transactions subject to the Electronic Fund Transfer Act. In particular, we apply this Section 4 - EFT Error Resolution Policy to:

Error claims regarding funds sent to or received by a consumer Customer to a Google Pay Balance from a debit card, or via ACH from a checking or savings account;
Error claims regarding funds reflected in a Google Pay account, Google Pay Balance, or disbursement account (as applicable) transferred by a consumer Customer to a debit card, or via ACH to a checking or savings account;
Error claims regarding funds transferred from a Google Pay Balance to a disbursement account;
Error claims regarding purchases paid for with a Google Pay Balance or GPBP Card held by a consumer Customer or any other Google Pay Balance, or GPBP Card transaction of the consumer Customer; and
Error claims on a Google Pay Balance held by a consumer Customer.

This EFT Error Resolution Policy DOES NOT apply to payments to a Seller through the Processing Service for Third Parties.

For Balance in Google Wallet balances and GPBP Card transactions, only the owner of the account (the designated Family Manager in Google Family Link) may submit disputes or file complaints / inquiries regarding the balance or related transactions or otherwise communicate with customer service regarding the balance or GPBP Card and related Card Account.

A. Notification to GPC of An Error on Your Google Pay Transaction or GPBP Card Transaction Subject To This Section 4 - EFT Error Resolution Policy

In the event that you think that there is an error in your Google Pay transaction, or GPBP Card transaction, or related to your Google Pay Balance subject to this Section 4 – EFT Error Resolution Policy, or you have questions or need more information about the Google Pay transaction, listed on a statement, transaction history or receipt, or GPBP Card transaction or your Google Pay Balance, please contact us as soon as you can by calling 1-888-986-7944, or by writing us at Google Payment Corp. – Complaints Handling P.O. Box 727, Mountain View, CA 94043. You can also dispute a GPBP Card transaction online by going to wallet.google.com and selecting “Report an issue” from the transaction you wish to dispute.

We must hear from you via one of the methods described above no later than 60 days after the earlier of the date you electronically access your account, if the error could be viewed in your electronic history, or the date we sent the FIRST written transaction history on which the problem or error appeared regarding the Google Pay transaction, or Google Pay Balance.

For Balance in Google Wallet balances and GPBP Card transactions, we must hear from you no later than 60 days after we made available to you the FIRST statement on which the problem or error appeared.

(1) Tell us your name and your email address associated with your Google Pay account, or, for GPBP Card transactions, your Card Account or GPBP Card number(s).

(2) Describe the error or the transfer you are unsure about and explain as clearly as you can why you believe it is an error or why you need more information.

(3) Tell us the dollar amount of the suspected error.

For your Google Pay Balance funded from a checking or savings account or from a debit card, we may extend this 60-day notification period if a delay resulted from your initial attempt to notify the financial institution holding your Funding Account.

If you tell us orally, we may require that you send us your complaint or question in writing within 10 business days.

We will determine whether an error occurred within 10 business days after we hear from you and will correct any error promptly. If we need more time, however, we may take up to 45 days to investigate your complaint or question. If we decide to do this, we will issue a provisional credit to your Google Pay Balance, funds held by us and reflected in your Google Pay account interface (if no activated Google Pay Balance), or Card Account (if related to a GPBP Card transaction) or Funding Account within 10 business days for the amount you think is in error, so that you will have the use of the money during the time it takes us to complete our investigation. If we ask you to put your complaint or question in writing and we do not receive it within 10 business days, we may not credit your account. For disputed GPBP Card transactions, your provisional credit will be added to your Card Account and then transferred to your Balance in Google Wallet, as described in your Google Pay Balance Prepaid Visa® Cardholder Agreement.

For errors involving new Google Pay accounts or GPBP Cards, or foreign-initiated transactions, we may take up to 90 days to investigate your complaint or question. For new Google Pay accounts or GPBP Cards, we may take up to 20 business days to issue a provisional credit to your Google Pay Balance, funds held by us and reflected in your Google Pay account interface (if no activated Google Pay Balance), Funding Account or Card Account (if related to a GPBP Card transaction) for the amount you think is in error.

We will tell you the results within 3 business days after completing our investigation. If we decide that there was no error, we will send you a written explanation by email or U.S. mail. If we had issued a provisional credit to your Google Pay Balance, funds held by us and reflected in your Google Pay account interface (if no activated Google Pay Balance), Funding Account for the error or Card Account (if related to a GPBP Card transaction),, such credit will be reversed and the written explanation will include notice of such reversal. You may ask for copies of the documents that we relied on in making our determination that there was no error.

If you need more information about our error resolution procedures, call us at the telephone number shown above.

ALL QUESTIONS ABOUT YOUR GPBP CARD TRANSACTIONS OR YOUR GOOGLE PAY BALANCE FUNDED FROM A CHECKING OR SAVINGS ACCOUNT OR FROM A DEBIT CARD MUST BE DIRECTED TO US, GPC, AND NOT TO THE FINANCIAL INSTITUTION THAT MAINTAINS THE FUNDING ACCOUNT. We are responsible for the Services and for resolving any errors in your Google Pay transaction subject to this Section 4 – EFT Error Resolution Policy.
